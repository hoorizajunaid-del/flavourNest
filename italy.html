Italian Desserts

Tiramisu
Ingredients:
  • Mascarpone cheese
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Espresso coffee
  • Ladyfinger biscuits
  • Cocoa powder
Method:

Whisk egg yolks with sugar, fold in mascarpone. Dip biscuits in coffee, layer with cream. Dust cocoa on top. Chill 4 hours.

Panna Cotta
Ingredients:
  • Heavy cream
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla
  • Gelatin
Method:

Heat cream with sugar and vanilla. Add gelatin. Pour into molds and refrigerate until set.

Cannoli
Ingredients:
  • Cannoli shells
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Powdered sugar
  • Chocolate chips
Method:

Mix ricotta with sugar and chocolate chips. Fill shells just before serving and dust with powdered sugar.

Gelato
Ingredients:
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Egg yolks
  • Flavoring (chocolate, vanilla, pistachio)
Method:

Heat milk and sugar, temper yolks, cook until thick. Add flavor. Freeze while stirring occasionally until creamy.

Biscotti
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Eggs
  • Almonds
  • Baking powder
Method:

Mix all ingredients into dough. Shape into logs, bake. Slice logs, bake again until crisp.

Zabaglione
Ingredients:
  • Egg yolks
  • Sugar
  • Sweet Marsala wine
Method:

Whisk egg yolks and sugar over simmering water. Slowly add Marsala. Whisk until thick and frothy. Serve warm.

Italian Pasta

Spaghetti Carbonara
Ingredients:
  • Spaghetti
  • Egg yolks
  • Pecorino cheese
  • Pancetta
  • Black pepper
Method:

Cook pasta. Fry pancetta. Mix eggs and cheese. Combine everything off heat for creamy sauce. Serve hot.

Lasagna
Ingredients:
  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
Method:

Layer pasta, meat sauce, ricotta, and mozzarella. Bake until golden and bubbly. Serve warm.

Pesto Pasta
Ingredients:
  • Pasta of choice
  • Basil leaves
  • Pine nuts
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
Method:

Blend basil, pine nuts, parmesan, and olive oil. Toss with cooked pasta and serve immediately.

Italian Dinner

Chicken Parmigiana
Ingredients:
  • Chicken breasts
  • Bread crumbs
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
Method:

Bread and fry chicken breasts. Place in baking dish, top with tomato sauce and mozzarella. Bake 20 minutes until cheese melts.

Risotto ai Funghi
Ingredients:
  • Arborio rice
  • Mushrooms
  • Onion
  • Vegetable broth
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Butter
Method:

Sauté onions and mushrooms in butter. Add rice, gradually add broth until creamy. Finish with Parmesan cheese and serve warm.

Eggplant Parmesan
Ingredients:
  • Eggplant slices
  • Bread crumbs
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
Method:

Bread and fry eggplant slices. Layer with tomato sauce and cheese in a baking dish. Bake 25-30 minutes until golden.

Mushroom Lasagna
Ingredients:
  • Lasagna sheets
  • Mushrooms
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Bechamel sauce
Method:

Layer pasta sheets, mushroom mixture, ricotta, mozzarella, and béchamel sauce. Bake 30 minutes until bubbling.

Italian Snacks

Bruschetta
Ingredients:
  • Bread slices
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Basil
  • Olive oil
Method:

Toast bread slices. Top with diced tomatoes, garlic, basil, and drizzle olive oil. Serve immediately.

Caprese Skewers
Ingredients:
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Mozzarella balls
  • Fresh basil
  • Balsamic glaze
Method:

Skewer tomatoes, mozzarella, and basil. Drizzle with balsamic glaze. Serve chilled.

Arancini
Ingredients:
  • Cooked rice
  • Mozzarella cubes
  • Bread crumbs
  • Eggs
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Form rice balls with mozzarella inside. Coat with egg and breadcrumbs. Fry until golden. Serve hot.

Focaccia
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Yeast
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Rosemary
Method:

Prepare dough, let rise. Press into a pan, drizzle olive oil, sprinkle rosemary and salt. Bake 20-25 minutes.
