Authentic Pakistani recipes from breakfast to snacks

Pakistani Breakfast

Halwa Puri
Ingredients:
  • Semolina
  • Flour
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Spices (cardamom, nutmeg)
  • Oil for frying puris
Method:

Prepare sweet semolina halwa by roasting semolina with ghee, adding sugar and spices. Roll puris and deep fry. Serve hot with halwa and spicy curry.

Chana Chaat
Ingredients:
  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Chopped onions
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chilies
  • Tamarind chutney
  • Lemon juice
Method:

Mix all ingredients in a bowl. Adjust seasoning with salt and chaat masala. Serve chilled as breakfast or snack.

Pakora
Ingredients:
  • Chickpea flour
  • Potatoes / Spinach / Onion
  • Spices (red chili, turmeric)
  • Water
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Mix flour with water and spices to make batter. Dip vegetables and deep fry until golden. Serve with chutney or ketchup.

Halwa Chana
Ingredients:
  • Chickpeas
  • Ghee
  • Sugar
  • Cardamom
Method:

Boil chickpeas, fry in ghee, add sugar and cardamom, and simmer until thick. Serve warm as breakfast delight.

Pakistani Lunch

Chicken Biryani
Ingredients:
  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken pieces
  • Yogurt
  • Spices (garam masala, turmeric, chili, coriander)
  • Onions, tomatoes
Method:

Marinate chicken with yogurt and spices. Fry onions and tomatoes, cook chicken. Layer with boiled rice and steam on low heat. Serve with raita.

Nihari
Ingredients:
  • Beef shank
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Onions
  • Spices (cumin, fennel, chili)
  • Flour (for thickening)
Method:

Cook beef with spices on low heat overnight. Mix flour with water to thicken. Serve hot with naan or steamed rice.

Daal Chawal
Ingredients:
  • Red lentils
  • Onion, garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Spices (turmeric, chili powder, cumin)
  • Rice
Method:

Cook lentils with onions, tomatoes, and spices. Serve hot with steamed rice and pickle on the side.

Seekh Kebab
Ingredients:
  • Minced beef or chicken
  • Onion, garlic, ginger
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili, garam masala)
  • Skewers
Method:

Mix minced meat with spices and shape on skewers. Grill or pan fry until cooked. Serve hot with naan and chutney.

Pakistani Dinner

Chicken Karahi
Ingredients:
  • Chicken pieces
  • Tomatoes
  • Green chilies
  • Garlic, ginger
  • Spices (red chili, coriander, cumin)
Method:

Fry chicken with garlic, ginger, and spices. Add tomatoes and cook until oil separates. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.

Beef Korma
Ingredients:
  • Beef cubes
  • Yogurt
  • Onions
  • Garlic, ginger
  • Spices (cardamom, cinnamon, cumin, chili)
Method:

Cook beef slowly in a yogurt-spice mixture until tender. Fry onions separately and mix in. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Beef Bunni
Ingredients:
  • Minced beef
  • Onion, garlic, ginger
  • Spices (garam masala, chili powder)
  • Tomatoes
Method:

Cook minced beef with spices, onions, garlic, and tomatoes. Simmer until thickened. Serve hot with naan.

Saag & Makki
Ingredients:
  • Spinach & mustard greens
  • Maize flour
  • Ghee
  • Garlic
  • Chili
Method:

Cook greens until soft, blend and simmer with ghee and spices. Serve hot with maize flatbreads (makki ki roti).

Pakistani Snacks

Pani Puri
Ingredients:
  • Semolina or wheat flour puris
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Tamarind chutney
  • Spiced mint water
Method:

Fill puris with potato-chickpea mixture. Dip in spicy tamarind-mint water and serve immediately for a crunchy, tangy snack.

Chaat
Ingredients:
  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Potatoes
  • Onions, tomatoes
  • Chutneys (tamarind, mint)
  • Chaat masala
Method:

Mix all ingredients, sprinkle chaat masala, drizzle chutneys. Serve chilled for a tangy, spicy treat.

Samosa
Ingredients:
  • Potatoes
  • Peas
  • Flour
  • Spices (cumin, chili, coriander)
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Make dough, roll and stuff with spiced potato-pea mixture. Shape into triangles and deep fry until golden. Serve hot with chutney.
