Gulab Jamun
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 4 tbsp milk (as needed)
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup water
  • 3-4 cardamom pods
  • Oil or ghee for frying
Method:

1. Mix milk powder, flour, baking soda, and ghee. Add milk gradually to form soft dough.
2. Make small smooth balls without cracks.
3. Heat oil on low-medium and fry balls until golden brown.
4. Boil sugar, water, and cardamom to make syrup (5 mins).
5. Soak fried balls in warm syrup for 2 hours.
6. Serve warm.

Serving Tip:

Serve with vanilla ice cream for a modern twist.

Butter Chicken
Ingredients:
  • 500g chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 1/2 cup cream
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
Method:

1. Marinate chicken with yogurt and spices for 1 hour.
2. Cook chicken in butter until lightly browned.
3. Add tomato puree and cook 10 minutes.
4. Add cream and garam masala.
5. Simmer 5 minutes.
6. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Serving Tip:

Garnish with fresh coriander and extra butter.

Chicken Biryani
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions sliced
  • 2 tomatoes chopped
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Few saffron strands
  • Fresh coriander & mint
Method:

1. Soak rice 30 mins, boil 70% cooked.
2. Fry onions until golden, remove half for garnish.
3. Add chicken, yogurt, spices, tomatoes. Cook 10 mins.
4. Layer rice over chicken.
5. Add saffron milk and fried onions.
6. Cover and cook on low flame 15 mins (dum method).
7. Mix gently and serve hot.

Serving Tip:

Best served with raita and salad.

Rasgulla
Ingredients:
  • 1 liter full cream milk
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp rose water (optional)
Method:

1. Boil milk. Add lemon juice slowly to curdle milk.
2. Strain through cloth and rinse with water.
3. Squeeze excess water and knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth.
4. Make small smooth balls.
5. Boil sugar and water in a wide pot.
6. Add balls, cover and cook 15 minutes on medium heat.
7. Add rose water and cool completely.

Serving Tip:

Chill before serving for best texture.

Palak Paneer
Ingredients:
  • 250g paneer cubes
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 onion chopped
  • 1 tomato chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • Salt & chili to taste
Method:

1. Blanch spinach 2 minutes, grind to paste.
2. Fry onions until golden.
3. Add ginger-garlic and tomato.
4. Add spices and cook well.
5. Add spinach puree and simmer 5 mins.
6. Add paneer cubes and cream.
7. Cook 5 more minutes.

Serving Tip:

Serve with naan or jeera rice.

Chana Masala
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups boiled chickpeas
  • 1 onion finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes pureed
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
Method:

1. Heat oil and add cumin seeds.
2. Add onion and fry until golden.
3. Add tomato puree and spices.
4. Cook until oil separates.
5. Add chickpeas and simmer 10 minutes.
6. Garnish with coriander.

Serving Tip:

Best served with bhature or rice.

Tandoori Chicken
Ingredients:
  • 1 whole chicken (cut)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp tandoori masala
  • 1 tsp chili powder
  • Salt to taste
Method:

1. Make deep cuts in chicken.
2. Mix yogurt, spices, lemon juice.
3. Marinate chicken 4–6 hours.
4. Bake at 200°C for 30–35 mins.
5. Broil 5 mins for char effect.

Serving Tip:

Serve with onion rings and mint chutney.

Samosa
Ingredients:
  • 2 boiled potatoes
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Flour for dough
  • Oil for frying
Method:

1. Prepare dough and rest 30 mins.
2. Cook potato-pea filling with spices.
3. Roll dough, fill and shape triangles.
4. Fry on medium-low heat until golden.

Serving Tip:

Serve hot with tamarind chutney.

Pav Bhaji
Ingredients:
  • 2 potatoes
  • 1/2 cup peas
  • 1/2 cup capsicum
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp pav bhaji masala
  • Pav bread
Method:

1. Boil vegetables and mash.
2. Fry onion and capsicum in butter.
3. Add tomatoes and masala.
4. Add mashed vegetables and cook 10 mins.
5. Toast pav in butter.

Serving Tip:

Top with butter cube and lemon.
