The Ultimate Biryani Guide

Types of Biryani

1. Hyderabadi Biryani

Known for its aromatic spices, saffron, and layered cooking method. Usually made with marinated chicken or mutton.

2. Sindhi Biryani

Spicy and tangy biryani with yogurt, tomatoes, and potatoes. Rich in flavors and often garnished with fried onions.

3. Karachi Biryani

Famous for its deep red color and spicy taste. Includes potatoes and often a hint of vinegar for tanginess.

4. Bombay Biryani

Aromatic and mildly spicy with potatoes and caramelized onions. Often served with raita.

5. Kolkata Biryani

Mildly spiced with potatoes and boiled eggs. Less oily but rich in flavors and aroma.

6. Malabar Biryani

Popular in Kerala, India. Uses short-grain rice, coconut milk, and fried onions with chicken or mutton.

Classic Chicken Biryani Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala powder
  • 4–5 green chilies
  • 1/2 cup fresh coriander & mint leaves
  • 4 cups water
  • 1/4 cup oil or ghee
  • Saffron soaked in milk (optional)

Method (Step by Step):

  1. Wash basmati rice thoroughly and soak for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry onions until golden brown. Remove half for garnish.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and sauté for 1 minute.
  4. Add chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  5. Add yogurt, red chili powder, turmeric, garam masala, salt, and green chilies. Cook 10–15 minutes.
  6. Boil 4 cups water in a large pot. Add soaked rice and cook until 70% done. Drain water.
  7. Layer partially cooked rice over chicken in a heavy-bottomed pot.
  8. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk on top.
  9. Cover tightly and cook on low flame for 25–30 minutes (dum cooking).
  10. Turn off heat and let it rest for 10 minutes.
  11. Gently fluff rice and serve hot with raita or salad.

Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken (cut into pieces)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • 4–5 green chilies
  • Saffron soaked in ¼ cup warm milk
  • Oil or ghee – ½ cup

Method (Step by Step):

  1. Fry onions in oil until golden brown. Set aside half for garnish.
  2. Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, green chilies, and half of fried onions. Marinate 1 hour.
  3. Boil rice until 70% cooked. Drain water.
  4. Layer chicken in a heavy-bottomed pot, add rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
  6. Cover tightly and cook on low flame (dum) for 30–35 minutes.
  7. Rest 10 minutes, gently fluff, serve hot with raita.

Sindhi Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken or mutton
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 potatoes (cubed)
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil – ½ cup

Method:

  1. Fry onions in oil until golden. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Add chicken/mutton, ginger garlic paste, and spices. Cook 10–15 minutes.
  3. Add tomatoes and potatoes, cook until tender.
  4. Boil rice until 70% done. Drain water.
  5. Layer meat & potatoes in pot, then rice on top.
  6. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, and ghee.
  7. Cook on low flame (dum) 30–35 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with salad or raita.

Karachi Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 2 potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • 1–2 tbsp vinegar (optional, for tang)
  • Oil – ½ cup

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden brown. Set aside half for garnish.
  2. Add chicken, potatoes, ginger garlic paste, and spices. Cook 10 minutes.
  3. Add yogurt and vinegar, simmer until chicken is tender.
  4. Boil rice until 70% done. Drain water.
  5. Layer chicken & potatoes, then rice on top.
  6. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, ghee.
  7. Dum cook on low heat 25–30 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with raita or salad.

Kolkata Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken or mutton

  • 2 onions (fried)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 boiled eggs
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil – ½ cup

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden brown. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, and half of fried onions. Marinate 1 hour.
  3. Boil rice until 70% cooked. Drain water.
  4. Layer chicken in pot, then rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle remaining onions, mint, coriander, and ghee.
  6. Place boiled eggs on top.
  7. Cook on low flame (dum) for 25–30 minutes.
  8. Serve hot with raita.

Malabar Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg chicken or mutton
  • 3 cups short-grain rice (Jeerakasala rice)
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup coconut milk
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil – ½ cup

Method:

  1. Fry onions in oil until golden. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Mix chicken with yogurt, spices, and half fried onions. Marinate 30–60 minutes.
  3. Boil rice until 70% done. Drain water.
  4. Layer chicken in heavy-bottomed pot, then rice on top.
  5. Pour coconut milk over rice. Sprinkle remaining onions, mint, coriander.
  6. Cover tightly and cook on low flame (dum) 25–30 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with raita or salad.

Beef Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg beef (cut into cubes)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • 4–5 green chilies
  • Oil or ghee – ½ cup
  • Saffron soaked in milk (optional)

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden brown. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Marinate beef with yogurt, spices, and half fried onions for 1–2 hours.
  3. Boil rice until 70% done. Drain water.
  4. Layer beef in a heavy-bottomed pot, then rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
  6. Cover tightly and cook on low flame (dum) 40–50 minutes until beef is tender.
  7. Serve hot with raita or salad.

Egg Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 6 boiled eggs
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil or ghee – ½ cup
  • Saffron soaked in milk (optional)

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Mix yogurt, spices, and half fried onions in a bowl.
  3. Boil rice until 70% cooked. Drain water.
  4. Layer marinated yogurt mixture and then rice in a pot.
  5. Place boiled eggs on top. Sprinkle remaining fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
  6. Cook on low flame (dum) 20–25 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with raita or salad.

Fish Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 500g fish fillets (boneless)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil – ½ cup
  • Lemon juice 1 tbsp
  • Saffron soaked in milk (optional)

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Marinate fish with yogurt, spices, lemon juice, and half fried onions for 15–20 minutes.
  3. Boil rice until 70% cooked. Drain water.
  4. Layer marinated fish in a pot, then rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
  6. Cook on low flame (dum) 15–20 minutes (fish cooks faster than meat).
  7. Serve hot with salad or raita.

Vegetable Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup carrots (chopped)
  • 1 cup beans (chopped)
  • 1 cup peas
  • 2 potatoes (cubed)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • Oil or ghee – ½ cup

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Cook all vegetables lightly in oil with ginger garlic paste and spices.
  3. Boil rice until 70% done. Drain water.
  4. Layer vegetables in pot, then rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk (optional), and ghee.
  6. Cook on low flame (dum) 20–25 minutes.
  7. Serve hot with raita or salad.

Mutton Biryani

Ingredients:

  • 1 kg mutton
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (sliced)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup mint & coriander leaves
  • 4–5 green chilies
  • Oil or ghee – ½ cup
  • Saffron soaked in milk (optional)

Method:

  1. Fry onions until golden brown. Keep half for garnish.
  2. Marinate mutton with yogurt, spices, half fried onions for 1–2 hours.
  3. Boil rice until 70% cooked. Drain water.
  4. Layer mutton in pot, then rice on top.
  5. Sprinkle fried onions, mint, coriander, saffron milk, and ghee.
  6. Cook on low flame (dum) 40–50 minutes until mutton is tender.
  7. Serve hot with raita, salad, or pickle.
