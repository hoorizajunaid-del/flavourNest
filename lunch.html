Delicious Pakistani Lunch

20 Traditional & Famous Recipes for Your Midday Meal

Popular Pakistani Lunch Recipes

Chicken Biryani
Ingredients:
  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken pieces
  • Yogurt & spices (garam masala, turmeric, chili, coriander)
  • Onions, tomatoes
  • Oil & ghee
Method:

Marinate chicken in yogurt & spices. Fry onions & tomatoes. Layer chicken and rice. Steam on low heat. Serve hot with raita.

Beef Nihari
Ingredients:
  • Beef shank
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Onions
  • Spices (cumin, fennel, chili)
  • Flour (for thickening)
Method:

Cook beef with spices on low heat overnight. Mix flour with water to thicken. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Chicken Karahi
Ingredients:
  • Chicken pieces
  • Tomatoes & green chilies
  • Garlic, ginger
  • Spices (red chili, coriander, cumin)
Method:

Fry chicken with garlic, ginger & spices. Add tomatoes & cook until oil separates. Garnish with fresh coriander & serve hot.

Daal Chawal
Ingredients:
  • Red lentils
  • Onion, garlic
  • Tomatoes
  • Spices (turmeric, chili powder, cumin)
  • Rice
Method:

Cook lentils with onions, tomatoes & spices. Serve hot with steamed rice & pickle.

Seekh Kebab
Ingredients:
  • Minced beef or chicken
  • Onion, garlic, ginger
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili, garam masala)
  • Skewers
Method:

Mix minced meat with spices & shape on skewers. Grill or pan-fry until cooked. Serve with naan & chutney.

Haleem
Ingredients:
  • Wheat, lentils & meat (chicken/beef)
  • Ginger, garlic
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili, turmeric)
  • Ghee
Method:

Cook wheat, lentils & meat together for several hours. Blend to smooth consistency. Fry spices in ghee & add. Serve hot.

Chapli Kebab
Ingredients:
  • Minced beef or lamb
  • Onion, tomatoes, green chilies
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili, garam masala)
  • Egg & flour (binding)
Method:

Mix all ingredients. Shape flat patties. Fry until golden. Serve with naan & salad.

Chicken Korma
Ingredients:
  • Chicken pieces
  • Yogurt & cream
  • Onions
  • Spices (cardamom, cinnamon, cumin)
Method:

Fry onions & spices. Add chicken & yogurt. Simmer until tender. Garnish with fried onions. Serve with naan.

Palak Gosht
Ingredients:
  • Spinach & lamb/beef
  • Onion, garlic, ginger
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili)
Method:

Cook meat with onions & spices. Add spinach & cook until wilted. Serve hot with roti or rice.

Karahi Paneer
Ingredients:
  • Paneer cubes
  • Tomatoes & bell peppers
  • Garlic, ginger
  • Spices (red chili, cumin, coriander)
Method:

Fry paneer lightly. Cook vegetables & spices. Add paneer. Serve with naan or paratha.

Beef Biryani
Ingredients:
  • Basmati rice
  • Beef pieces
  • Yogurt & spices (garam masala, turmeric, chili, coriander)
  • Onions, tomatoes
  • Oil & ghee
Method:

Marinate beef in yogurt & spices. Fry onions & tomatoes. Layer beef and rice. Steam on low heat. Serve hot with raita.

Chicken Shawarma
Ingredients:
  • Chicken breast or thighs
  • Garlic, lemon, yogurt
  • Spices (paprika, cumin, turmeric)
  • Flatbreads
  • Salad & garlic sauce
Method:

Marinate chicken in spices & yogurt. Grill or pan-fry. Slice and wrap in flatbread with salad & sauce. Serve warm.

Aloo Keema
Ingredients:
  • Minced beef or lamb
  • Potatoes
  • Onions, garlic, ginger
  • Tomatoes & green chilies
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili powder)
Method:

Fry onions, garlic, ginger. Add keema & spices. Cook until brown. Add potatoes & tomatoes. Simmer until tender. Serve hot.

Bhindi Masala
Ingredients:
  • Okra (bhindi)
  • Onion, garlic, ginger
  • Tomatoes
  • Spices (turmeric, chili, cumin)
  • Oil
Method:

Fry onions & spices. Add okra & tomatoes. Cook until tender. Serve with roti or paratha.

Mutton Pulao
Ingredients:
  • Basmati rice
  • Mutton pieces
  • Yogurt & spices (garam masala, cinnamon, cardamom)
  • Onions & ghee
Method:

Cook mutton with spices. Fry onions & layer with rice. Steam on low heat. Serve hot with salad.

Chana Masala
Ingredients:
  • Chickpeas (soaked overnight)
  • Onions, tomatoes, garlic, ginger
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, chili, turmeric)
  • Oil
Method:

Cook chickpeas until tender. Fry onion & spices. Add tomatoes & chickpeas. Simmer for 10–15 mins. Serve with roti.

Lahori Fried Fish
Ingredients:
  • Fish fillets
  • Gram flour & spices (turmeric, chili, salt)
  • Lemon juice
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Marinate fish with spices & lemon. Coat with gram flour. Deep fry until golden. Serve with chutney or salad.

Egg Curry
Ingredients:
  • Boiled eggs
  • Onions, garlic, ginger
  • Tomatoes & green chilies
  • Spices (cumin, coriander, turmeric, chili)
  • Oil
Method:

Fry onion & spices. Add tomatoes & simmer. Add boiled eggs. Cook 5 mins. Garnish & serve with rice or roti.

Vegetable Pulao
Ingredients:
  • Basmati rice
  • Mixed vegetables (carrot, peas, beans)
  • Onion & garlic
  • Spices (cumin, cardamom, cloves)
  • Oil or ghee
Method:

Sauté onions & vegetables. Add rice & spices. Cook in water until fluffy. Serve hot with raita.

Masoor Daal
Ingredients:
  • Red lentils
  • Onions, garlic, ginger
  • Tomatoes
  • Spices (turmeric, chili, cumin)
  • Oil or ghee
Method:

Cook lentils with onions, tomatoes, & spices. Simmer until tender. Serve with steamed rice or chapati.

International Lunch Recipes

Italian Lasagna
Ingredients:
  • Lasagna sheets
  • Ground beef or chicken
  • Tomato sauce & garlic
  • Cheese (mozzarella & parmesan)
  • Herbs (basil, oregano)
Method:

Cook meat with tomato sauce & herbs. Layer sheets, meat, and cheese. Bake 30–40 mins until golden. Serve hot.

Greek Salad with Chicken
Ingredients:
  • Grilled chicken breast
  • Tomatoes, cucumbers, bell peppers
  • Feta cheese
  • Olives & red onion
  • Olive oil & lemon dressing
Method:

Mix vegetables, feta, and olives. Slice grilled chicken on top. Drizzle with olive oil & lemon. Serve fresh.

Mexican Tacos
Ingredients:
  • Soft corn tortillas
  • Grilled meat or chicken
  • Lettuce, tomatoes, onions
  • Cheese & salsa
  • Avocado (optional)
Method:

Fill tortillas with meat, veggies, and cheese. Top with salsa & avocado. Fold and serve immediately.

Japanese Sushi Bowl
Ingredients:
  • Steamed rice
  • Raw salmon or tuna
  • Avocado & cucumber
  • Soy sauce & sesame seeds
  • Seaweed strips
Method:

Layer rice, fish, and veggies in a bowl. Drizzle with soy sauce. Garnish with sesame & seaweed. Serve chilled.

Indian Butter Chicken
Ingredients:
  • Chicken pieces
  • Tomato puree & cream
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Spices (garam masala, chili, turmeric)
  • Butter & oil
Method:

Cook chicken in spices. Add tomato puree & cream. Simmer until thick. Serve hot with naan or rice.

French Quiche Lorraine
Ingredients:
  • Shortcrust pastry
  • Bacon or ham
  • Eggs & cream
  • Gruyere cheese
  • Nutmeg, salt & pepper
Method:

Place bacon & cheese in pastry. Mix eggs & cream, pour over. Bake 30–35 mins. Serve warm or cold.

American BLT Sandwich
Ingredients:
  • Bread slices
  • Bacon strips
  • Lettuce & tomato
  • Mayonnaise
  • Salt & pepper
Method:

Cook bacon until crispy. Toast bread. Spread mayo, add bacon, lettuce, tomato. Serve immediately.

Thai Green Curry with Rice
Ingredients:
  • Chicken or tofu
  • Coconut milk & green curry paste
  • Vegetables (bell pepper, zucchini)
  • Basil & lime leaves
  • Steamed jasmine rice
Method:

Cook protein & vegetables in coconut milk with curry paste. Garnish with basil. Serve over jasmine rice.

Spanish Paella
Ingredients:
  • Basmati or short-grain rice
  • Seafood or chicken
  • Bell peppers & peas
  • Saffron & paprika
  • Olive oil & garlic
Method:

Sauté vegetables & protein. Add rice & spices. Cook with stock until rice absorbs liquid. Serve hot.

Lebanese Falafel Wrap
Ingredients:
  • Falafel (chickpea patties)
  • Flatbread or pita
  • Lettuce, tomato, cucumber
  • Tahini sauce
  • Pickles
Method:

Place falafel in flatbread with vegetables & tahini. Roll tightly. Serve fresh with pickles.
