Delicious Pakistani Dinner Recipes

End your day with traditional and flavorful dishes

Chicken Karahi

Cooking Time: 40 Minutes

Servings: 4-5 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg chicken (cut pieces)
  • 4 medium tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup cooking oil
  • 2 green chilies (sliced)
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
Method:
  1. Heat oil in a karahi on medium flame.
  2. Add chicken pieces and fry until they change color.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add chopped tomatoes, salt, and red chili powder.
  5. Cook until tomatoes soften and oil separates.
  6. Sprinkle garam masala and cook for 5 more minutes.
  7. Garnish with green chilies and fresh coriander.
  8. Serve hot with naan or roti.

Beef Biryani

Cooking Time: 1 Hour 30 Minutes

Servings: 6 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg beef (medium pieces)
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 2 onions (thinly sliced & fried)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 potatoes (cut into halves)
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 2 tomatoes (chopped)
  • 2 green chilies
  • Fresh coriander & mint leaves
Method:
  1. Heat oil and fry onions until golden brown. Remove half for layering.
  2. Add beef and cook until it changes color.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste, tomatoes, yogurt, and biryani masala.
  4. Cook until beef becomes tender and oil separates.
  5. Boil rice in salted water until 70% cooked, then drain.
  6. In a large pot, layer rice over beef curry.
  7. Add fried onions, green chilies, coriander, and mint.
  8. Cover tightly and steam (dum) on low heat for 20 minutes.
  9. Mix gently and serve hot.

Mutton Korma

Cooking Time: 1 Hour 20 Minutes

Servings: 5-6 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg mutton
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • ½ cup oil
  • 2 tbsp korma masala
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp garam masala
Method:
  1. Heat oil and fry onions until golden brown.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add mutton and cook until it changes color.
  4. Add yogurt, salt, chili powder, and korma masala.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat until mutton becomes tender.
  6. Cook until oil separates from gravy.
  7. Sprinkle garam masala and serve hot with naan.

Nihari

Cooking Time: 3-4 Hours

Servings: 6 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg beef shank (with bone)
  • 2 tbsp nihari masala
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • ½ cup oil
  • 6 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt (or to taste)
  • 1 tbsp flour (for thickening)
  • Ginger slices, coriander & lemon for garnish
Method:
  1. Heat oil in a large pot and add beef. Cook until slightly browned.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add nihari masala, salt, and water. Mix well.
  4. Cover and cook on low flame for 3-4 hours until meat is tender.
  5. Mix flour with water and add to thicken the gravy.
  6. Cook another 10 minutes until gravy thickens.
  7. Garnish with ginger slices, coriander, and lemon.
  8. Serve hot with naan.

Chicken Pulao

Cooking Time: 50 Minutes

Servings: 5 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg chicken
  • 3 cups basmati rice (soaked 30 minutes)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • Whole spices (bay leaf, cloves, cardamom)
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 4 cups water
Method:
  1. Heat oil and fry sliced onion until golden.
  2. Add whole spices and ginger garlic paste.
  3. Add chicken and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add salt and water. Bring to boil.
  5. Add soaked rice and cook on medium flame.
  6. Cover and cook on low heat until rice is fully done.
  7. Fluff gently and serve hot.

Haleem

Cooking Time: 2-3 Hours

Servings: 6-8 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg beef (boneless)
  • 1 cup mixed lentils (chana, masoor, moong)
  • 1 cup broken wheat
  • 2 tbsp haleem masala
  • 1 onion (fried)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • ½ cup oil
  • Fresh coriander, ginger & lemon for garnish
Method:
  1. Boil lentils and broken wheat until soft.
  2. Cook beef with ginger garlic paste, salt, and haleem masala until tender.
  3. Shred the beef properly.
  4. Mix lentils mixture with shredded beef.
  5. Cook together on low heat while stirring until thick and smooth.
  6. Top with fried onion, ginger, coriander, and lemon.
  7. Serve hot.

Chapli Kabab

Cooking Time: 35 Minutes

Servings: 4-5 People

Ingredients:
  • ½ kg minced beef
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 tomato (finely chopped)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander
Method:
  1. Mix minced beef with onion, tomato, egg, spices, and coriander.

  5. Serve hot with naan or raita.

Palak Gosht

Cooking Time: 1 Hour 15 Minutes

Servings: 5 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg mutton
  • 500g spinach (washed & chopped)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ cup oil
  • 1 tsp garam masala
Method:
  1. Heat oil and fry onions until golden.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add mutton, salt, chili powder, and turmeric. Cook well.
  4. Cover and cook until mutton becomes tender.
  5. Add chopped spinach and cook on medium heat.
  6. Cook until oil separates and spinach blends well.

Daal Chawal

Cooking Time: 40 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup lentils (masoor or moong)
  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
Method:
  1. Wash lentils and boil with turmeric and salt until soft.
  2. Heat oil in a pan and fry onion until golden.
  3. Add tomatoes and chili powder. Cook until soft.
  4. Mix this masala into boiled daal and simmer for 5 minutes.
  5. Cook rice separately in salted water.
  6. Serve hot daal with steamed rice.

Chicken Tikka Masala

Cooking Time: 1 Hour

Servings: 5 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg boneless chicken (cubes)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp tikka masala
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • ½ cup fresh cream
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Fresh coriander for garnish
Method:
  1. Marinate chicken with yogurt, ginger garlic paste, salt, and tikka masala for 30 minutes.
  2. Grill or pan-fry chicken until cooked.
  3. In another pan, heat oil and add tomato puree.
  4. Cook until oil separates.
  5. Add cooked chicken and mix well.
  6. Add cream and simmer for 5-7 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander and serve hot.

Beef Kofta

Cooking Time: 1 Hour

Servings: 5 People

Ingredients:
  • ½ kg minced beef
  • 1 onion (grated)
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 cup tomato puree (for gravy)
  • ½ tsp garam masala
Method:
  1. Mix minced beef with grated onion, egg, ginger garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, and salt.
  2. Shape mixture into small balls (koftas).
  3. Heat oil in a pan and shallow fry koftas until golden. Remove and set aside.
  4. In the same pan, add tomato puree and cook until oil separates.
  5. Add koftas into the gravy and simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Sprinkle garam masala and serve hot with naan or rice.

Mix Sabzi

Cooking Time: 35 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup carrot (chopped)
  • 1 cup beans (chopped)
  • 1 cup peas
  • 1 cup potato (cubed)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
Method:
  1. Heat oil in a pan and fry onions until golden.
  2. Add ginger garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add all vegetables, turmeric, chili powder, and salt.
  4. Stir and cover. Cook on medium heat until vegetables are tender.
  5. Remove the lid and cook 5 more minutes to evaporate extra water.
  6. Serve hot with roti or rice.

Bhindi Masala

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

  • 500g okra (bhindi, chopped)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 tomato (chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
Method:
  1. Heat oil in a pan and fry okra on medium heat until slightly crisp. Remove and set aside.

Spaghetti Carbonara

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g pancetta or bacon (chopped)
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup grated parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Salt & black pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
Method:
  1. Cook spaghetti in salted water until al dente. Drain and reserve ½ cup pasta water.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan, fry garlic and pancetta until golden.
  3. Beat eggs with parmesan, salt, and pepper.
  4. Add hot spaghetti to the pan and remove from heat.
  5. Quickly mix in egg-cheese mixture, using reserved pasta water to make creamy.
  6. Serve immediately with extra parmesan and black pepper.

Sushi Rolls (Maki)

Cooking Time: 50 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 2 cups sushi rice (cooked)
  • 4 sheets nori
  • 1 cucumber (julienned)
  • 1 avocado (sliced)
  • 200g cooked shrimp or crab sticks
  • Soy sauce, wasabi, pickled ginger for serving
Method:
  1. Place nori sheet on bamboo mat, spread sushi rice evenly, leaving 1 inch at top.
  2. Layer cucumber, avocado, and shrimp on rice.
  3. Roll tightly using bamboo mat, seal edge with water.
  4. Cut into 6–8 pieces with a sharp knife.
  5. Serve with soy sauce, wasabi, and pickled ginger.

Pad Thai

Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 200g rice noodles
  • 150g shrimp or chicken
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp tamarind paste
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 2 tbsp peanuts (crushed)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Lime wedges for serving
Method:
  1. Cook rice noodles as per package instructions. Drain.
  2. Heat oil in a pan, sauté garlic, and shrimp/chicken until cooked.
  3. Push to side and scramble eggs in the same pan.
  4. Add noodles, fish sauce, and tamarind paste. Mix well.
  5. Add bean sprouts and toss for 2 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with crushed peanuts and lime wedges.

Tacos al Pastor

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 500g pork or chicken, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp adobo sauce
  • 1 pineapple (sliced)
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • Fresh coriander
  • Salt & pepper to taste
Method:
  1. Marinate meat with adobo sauce, salt, and pepper for 20 minutes.

  5. Serve hot with lime wedges.

Falafel Wrap

Cooking Time: 35 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup chickpeas (soaked overnight)
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Oil for frying
  • 4 pita breads
  • Lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and tahini sauce
Method:
  1. Blend soaked chickpeas, onion, garlic, cumin, coriander, salt, and pepper into a coarse mixture.
  2. Shape into small balls or patties.
  3. Deep-fry or shallow-fry until golden brown.
  4. Warm pita bread, fill with falafel, lettuce, tomato, cucumber, and drizzle with tahini sauce.
  5. Serve immediately.

Butter Chicken

Cooking Time: 50 Minutes

Servings: 5 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 kg chicken (boneless)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion (chopped)
  • 2 tomatoes (pureed)
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup cream
Method:
  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, salt, and chili powder for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat butter in a pan, fry onion until golden.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and cook 1 minute.
  4. Add tomato puree, turmeric, and simmer until oil separates.
  5. Add chicken and cook until tender.
  6. Stir in cream and cook 5 more minutes.
  7. Serve hot with naan or rice.

Ratatouille

Cooking Time: 45 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 1 zucchini (sliced)
  • 1 eggplant (sliced)
  • 1 red bell pepper (sliced)
  • 1 yellow bell pepper (sliced)
  • 2 tomatoes (sliced)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, thyme to taste
Method:
  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  3. Layer sliced vegetables in a baking dish alternately.
  4. Season with salt, pepper, and thyme.
  5. Cover with foil and bake 35 minutes.
  6. Serve hot or warm as a light lunch.

Chicken Teriyaki

Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

Servings: 4 People

Ingredients:
  • 500g chicken breast (sliced)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp mirin (or honey)
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 clove garlic (minced)
  • 1 tsp ginger (grated)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Spring onions for garnish
Method:
  1. Mix soy sauce, mirin, sugar, garlic, and ginger in a bowl.
  2. Marinate chicken for 10–15 minutes.
  3. Heat oil in a pan, cook chicken until golden and cooked through.
  4. Add remaining sauce and simmer until thickened.
  5. Garnish with spring onions and serve with steamed rice.
