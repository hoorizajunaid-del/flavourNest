Biryani

Biryani – The Heart of Pakistani Cuisine 🍛

Biryani is one of the most loved and iconic dishes of Pakistan. It is enjoyed at breakfast, lunch, dinner, and even as a special snack. The rich aroma of spices, tender meat, and flavorful rice makes biryani a favorite comfort food for every food lover.

From street-side stalls to family dinners and weddings, biryani brings people together with its unforgettable taste and vibrant colors.
