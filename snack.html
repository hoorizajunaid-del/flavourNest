International Snack Recipes - Radiant Theme

Bruschetta (Italy)
Ingredients: Baguette slices, tomatoes, garlic, basil, olive oil, salt.
Recipe: Toast bread, top with chopped tomato, basil, garlic, drizzle olive oil, serve.
Spring Rolls (China)
Ingredients: Spring roll wrappers, cabbage, carrot, soy sauce, oil.
Recipe: Sauté veggies with soy sauce, fill wrappers, roll, deep fry until golden.
Samosa (India)
Ingredients: Pastry, potatoes, peas, spices, oil.
Recipe: Fill pastry with spiced potatoes & peas, fold triangles, fry until golden.
Guacamole & Chips (Mexico)
Ingredients: Avocado, lime, onion, tomato, tortilla chips, salt.
Recipe: Mash avocado with lime, onion, tomato, salt; serve with chips.
Falafel (Middle East)
Ingredients: Chickpeas, garlic, parsley, cumin, coriander, oil.
Recipe: Blend chickpeas with spices, shape into balls, fry until crispy.
Mini Quiches (France)
Ingredients: Puff pastry, eggs, cream, cheese, spinach or bacon.
Recipe: Fill pastry with egg mixture, bake at 180°C for 20 min.
Mozzarella Sticks (USA)
Ingredients: Mozzarella, breadcrumbs, eggs, oil, salt.
Recipe: Coat cheese sticks with egg & breadcrumbs, freeze 30 min, deep fry until golden.
Empanadas (Argentina)
Ingredients: Pastry, beef/chicken, onion, bell pepper, spices.
Recipe: Cook filling, stuff pastry, fold, bake or fry until golden.
Tempura Veggies (Japan)
Ingredients: Vegetables, tempura flour, ice water, oil, salt.
Recipe: Dip veggies in tempura batter, deep fry until crispy, serve hot.
Stuffed Mushrooms (Italy)
Ingredients: Mushrooms, cream cheese, garlic, herbs, breadcrumbs.
Recipe: Stuff mushrooms with cheese mixture, sprinkle breadcrumbs, bake at 180°C for 15 min.
Banana Bread
Ingredients: Bananas, flour, sugar, eggs, butter, baking soda.
Recipe: Mash bananas, mix with other ingredients, bake at 180°C for 50 min.
Chocolate Chip Cookies
Ingredients: Flour, sugar, butter, eggs, chocolate chips, baking powder.
Recipe: Mix all ingredients, shape cookies, bake at 175°C for 12 min.
Blueberry Muffins
Ingredients: Flour, sugar, butter, eggs, milk, blueberries, baking powder.
Recipe: Fold blueberries into batter, pour into muffin cups, bake at 180°C for 25 min.
Cheese Croissants
Ingredients: Puff pastry, cheese slices, egg wash.
Recipe: Wrap cheese in pastry, brush with egg, bake at 200°C for 15-20 min.
Apple Pie
Ingredients: Apples, sugar, cinnamon, butter, flour, pie crust.
Recipe: Fill crust with apple mixture, cover top, bake at 180°C for 45 min.
Brownies
Ingredients: Butter, chocolate, sugar, eggs, flour.

Cinnamon Rolls
Ingredients: Flour, sugar, butter, cinnamon, eggs, yeast.
Recipe: Roll dough with cinnamon sugar, cut, bake at 180°C for 20-25 min.
Pumpkin Bread
Ingredients: Pumpkin puree, flour, sugar, eggs, butter, baking soda.
Recipe: Mix ingredients, pour into loaf pan, bake at 180°C for 50 min.
Lemon Drizzle Cake
Ingredients: Flour, sugar, butter, eggs, lemon juice, zest, baking powder.

Garlic Bread
Ingredients: Baguette, butter, garlic, parsley.

Focaccia Bread
