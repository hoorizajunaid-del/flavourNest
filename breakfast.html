Most Famous Breakfasts Around The World 🍳

Start your day with flavors from every culture

1. American Pancakes (USA)
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
Method:

Mix dry ingredients separately. Whisk eggs and milk. Combine both mixtures. Cook on a greased pan until golden. Serve with maple syrup.

2. Full English Breakfast (UK)
Ingredients:
  • Eggs
  • Sausages
  • Bacon
  • Beans
  • Toast
Method:

Fry sausages and bacon. Cook eggs as desired. Warm baked beans. Serve hot with buttered toast.

3. Croissant (France)
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Butter
  • Milk
  • Yeast
  • Sugar
Method:

Prepare laminated dough with butter layers. Shape into crescents and bake until golden and flaky.

4. Shakshuka (Middle East)
Ingredients:
  • Tomatoes
  • Eggs
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Spices
Method:

Cook tomatoes with onion and spices. Crack eggs into sauce and cook until set. Serve with bread.

5. Paratha & Chai (Pakistan/India)
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Water
  • Salt
  • Ghee
Method:

Knead dough. Roll and cook on pan with ghee. Serve hot with tea and yogurt.

6. Spanish Omelette (Spain)
Ingredients:
  • Eggs
  • Potatoes
  • Onions
Method:

Fry potatoes and onions. Add beaten eggs and cook until firm.

7. Sushi Breakfast (Japan)
Ingredients:
  • Rice
  • Fish
  • Seaweed
Method:

Prepare sushi rice. Add fish and roll tightly. Slice and serve.

8. Dim Sum (China)
Ingredients:
  • Dumpling wrappers
  • Chicken
  • Vegetables
Method:

Fill wrappers and steam until cooked.

9. Bagel & Cream Cheese (USA)
Ingredients:
  • Bagel
  • Cream cheese
Method:

Slice and toast bagel. Spread cream cheese generously.

10. Avocado Toast (Australia)
Ingredients:
  • Bread
  • Avocado
  • Lemon
Method:

Mash avocado with lemon. Spread over toasted bread.

11. Huevos Rancheros (Mexico)
Ingredients:
  • Tortilla
  • Eggs
  • Salsa
Method:

Fry eggs and serve over tortilla topped with salsa.

12. Congee (China)
Ingredients:
  • Rice
  • Water
  • Chicken
Method:

Cook rice with water until porridge consistency.

13. Crepes (France)
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
Method:

Make thin batter and cook lightly on pan.

14. Idli & Sambar (India)
Ingredients:
  • Rice batter
  • Lentils
Method:

Steam batter and serve with lentil soup.

15. Waffles (Belgium)
Ingredients:
  • Flour
  • Eggs
  • Milk
Method:

Pour batter into waffle iron and cook until crispy.

French Toast
Ingredients:
  • 4 slices thick bread (brioche or white)
  • 2 eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter for frying
  • Maple syrup or honey
Method:

Whisk eggs, milk, sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla. Dip bread slices and fry in butter until golden. Serve with syrup or fruits.

Miso Soup Breakfast
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tbsp miso paste
  • ½ cup tofu cubes
  • 1 tbsp chopped green onions
  • 1 tsp wakame (seaweed)
Method:

Heat water, dissolve miso paste. Add tofu and seaweed. Simmer 2–3 mins. Garnish with green onions and serve warm.

Breakfast Burrito
Ingredients:
  • 2 flour tortillas
  • 3 eggs
  • ½ cup cooked sausage or chicken
  • ¼ cup bell peppers
  • ¼ cup onions
  • ½ cup shredded cheese
  • Salt & pepper
  • Salsa (optional)
Method:

Scramble eggs. Cook veggies and meat. Warm tortillas. Add mixture, fold, grill lightly. Serve warm.

Smoothie Bowl
Ingredients:
  • 1 frozen banana
  • ½ cup frozen berries
  • ½ cup yogurt or almond milk
  • Toppings: granola, chia seeds, fruits
Method:

Blend banana, berries, and yogurt. Pour into bowl. Add toppings. Serve immediately.

Granola & Yogurt
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • ½ cup granola
  • Fresh fruits
  • Honey (optional)
Method:

Layer yogurt, granola, and fruits in a bowl. Drizzle honey. Serve chilled.

Turkish Breakfast
Ingredients:
  • Eggs
  • Olives
  • Tomatoes & cucumbers
  • Feta cheese
  • Bread
  • Honey & butter
Method:

Fry or boil eggs. Arrange olives, cheese, and veggies. Serve with bread, honey, and butter. Enjoy Turkish tea.

Brazilian Pão de Queijo
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup tapioca flour
  • ½ cup milk
  • ¼ cup oil
  • 1 egg
  • ½ cup grated cheese
  • Salt
Method:

Heat milk and oil. Mix flour, salt, and cheese. Add egg and milk mixture. Shape small balls. Bake at 180°C for 15–20 minutes. Serve warm.

German Brötchen
Ingredients:
  • 3 cups flour
  • 1 tsp yeast
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup warm water
Method:

Mix yeast, sugar, and water. Add flour and salt. Knead and let rise 1 hour. Shape rolls and bake at 200°C for 20 minutes. Serve with butter and jam.

Nigerian Akara
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup black-eyed peas (soaked & peeled)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 chili
  • Salt
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Blend peas, onion, chili, and salt. Whisk mixture to add air. Deep fry spoonfuls until golden. Serve hot.

Ethiopian Injera
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups teff flour
  • 2½ cups water
  • Salt
Method:

Mix flour and water. Ferment 1–2 days. Pour batter on hot pan in circular motion. Cook without flipping. Serve with stews.

Russian Syrniki
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • Oil for frying
Method:

Mix all ingredients. Shape small patties. Fry until golden on both sides. Serve with sour cream or jam.

Canadian Poutine Breakfast
Ingredients:
  • French fries
  • Cheese curds
  • Gravy
  • Fried egg
  • Bacon
Method:

Cook fries. Add cheese curds. Pour hot gravy. Top with fried egg and bacon. Serve hot.

Korean Gyeran Bap
Ingredients:
  • 1 bowl steamed rice
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Green onions
Method:

Fry egg sunny side up. Place over hot rice. Add soy sauce and sesame oil. Mix and enjoy.

Thai Jok
Ingredients:
  • 1 cup rice
  • 4 cups water
  • ½ cup minced pork
  • Garlic
  • Soy sauce
Method:

Cook rice with water until very soft. Add pork and garlic. Season with soy sauce. Serve hot.

Italian Frittata
Ingredients:
  • 6 eggs
  • ½ cup vegetables (spinach, peppers)
  • ½ cup cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt & pepper
Method:

Beat eggs with salt and pepper. Cook vegetables. Pour eggs over vegetables. Cook on low until set. Finish in oven 5 minutes. Slice and serve.
