🌙 Ramadan Special Recipes

Explore delicious recipes for Iftar & Sehri

Chicken Biryani

Traditional spicy layered rice dish cooked with chicken, yogurt, aromatic spices and dum style cooking.

Beef Karahi

Rich tomato-based Pakistani karahi cooked with tender beef, green chilies and fresh coriander.

Haleem

Slow-cooked blend of wheat, lentils and meat, topped with fried onions and lemon.

Alfredo Bites

Mix cooked pasta with cream & Parmesan, shape into bites, bake at 180°C for 10 min.

Creamy Cheese Pie

Blend cream cheese, eggs, and sugar, pour into crust, bake 25-30 min at 175°C.

Samosa

Stuff pastry with spiced potatoes & peas, fold into triangles, deep fry till golden.

Pakora Piyaz

Mix sliced onions with chickpea flour, spices, and water, deep fry until crispy.

Pakora Aloo

Coat potato slices with spiced chickpea flour batter and deep fry until golden.

Fruit Chat

Mix chopped fruits with chaat masala, lemon juice, and mint. Serve fresh.

Dahi Baale

Soak fried lentil balls in yogurt, top with chutneys, chili, and coriander.

Freesko

Blend ice, milk, sugar, and fruits of choice. Serve chilled.

Bread & Cheese

Layer bread with cheese, butter lightly, and grill or toast till golden.

Chicken Cheese Pockets
Ingredients:
  • Chicken, cooked & shredded - 1 cup
  • Cheese, grated - 1/2 cup
  • Puff pastry sheets - 4
  • Salt, pepper, herbs - to taste
Recipe:

Mix chicken and cheese with seasoning, stuff into pastry, seal, bake 20 min at 180°C until golden.

Bread Roll
Ingredients:
  • Bread slices - 6
  • Boiled vegetables or chicken - 1 cup
  • Cheese - 1/2 cup
  • Egg - 1 (for coating)
Recipe:

Flatten bread, add filling, roll, coat in egg, deep fry until golden.

Chicken Lollipop
Ingredients:
  • Chicken wings - 500g
  • Garlic, ginger paste - 1 tbsp
  • Soy sauce, chili sauce - 2 tbsp each
  • Flour & cornflour mix - 1/2 cup
Recipe:

Marinate chicken, coat with flour mix, deep fry until crispy. Serve hot.

Macaroni
Ingredients:
  • Macaroni - 2 cups
  • Cheese - 1 cup
  • Milk - 1 cup
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Salt, pepper - to taste
Recipe:

Boil macaroni, melt butter, add milk and cheese, mix well, cook until creamy.

Spaghetti
Ingredients:
  • Spaghetti - 200g
  • Tomato sauce - 1 cup
  • Olive oil, garlic - 1 tbsp each
  • Salt, pepper, herbs - to taste
Recipe:

Boil spaghetti, sauté garlic in oil, add sauce, mix with spaghetti, serve hot.

Spring Roll
Ingredients:
  • Spring roll sheets - 6
  • Vegetables chopped - 1 cup
  • Soy sauce - 1 tbsp
  • Oil for frying
Recipe:

Cook vegetables with sauce, fill sheets, roll tightly, deep fry until crisp.

Keema Samosa
Ingredients:
  • Minced meat - 1 cup
  • Onion, chopped - 1
  • Spices - salt, pepper, chili powder
  • Pastry sheets - 6
Recipe:

Cook meat with onions & spices, fill sheets, fold into triangles, fry till golden.

Chocolate Covered Dates
Ingredients:
  • Dates - 12
  • Chocolate - 100g, melted
Recipe:

Stuff dates if desired, dip in melted chocolate, refrigerate until set.

Stuffed Dates
Ingredients:
  • Dates - 12
  • Almonds or walnuts - 12
  • Optional: Cream cheese - 2 tbsp
Recipe:

Open dates, stuff with nuts & cheese, close, serve fresh or chilled.

Baked Dates
Ingredients:
  • Dates - 12
  • Butter - 1 tbsp
  • Honey - 1 tsp
Recipe:

Preheat oven to 180°C, brush dates with butter & honey, bake 10 min, serve warm.

Mint Mojito
Ingredients:
  • Mint leaves - 10-12
  • Lemon juice - 2 tbsp
  • Sugar - 1 tsp
  • Soda water - 1 cup
  • Ice cubes - as needed
Recipe:

Muddle mint with sugar & lemon juice, add ice, pour soda water, stir gently, serve chilled.

Sabudana Drink
Ingredients:
  • Sabudana (tapioca pearls) - 2 tbsp
  • Milk - 1 cup
  • Sugar - 1 tsp
  • Ice cubes - optional
Recipe:

Soak sabudana for 30 min, boil in milk with sugar until soft, chill, serve with ice if desired.

Cold Coffee
Ingredients:
  • Milk - 1 cup
  • Coffee powder - 1 tsp
  • Sugar - 1-2 tsp
  • Ice cubes - 4-5
  • Whipped cream - optional
Recipe:

Blend milk, coffee, sugar, and ice until frothy. Pour into a glass, top with whipped cream.

Arabian Pudding
Ingredients:
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup
  • Cornflour - 2 tbsp
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Rose water - 1 tsp
Recipe:

Mix cornflour in milk, cook with sugar on low heat until thick, add cardamom & rose water, chill before serving.

Custard Triple
Ingredients:
  • Custard powder - 2 tbsp
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Sugar - 1/4 cup
  • Mixed fruits - 1 cup
Recipe:

Cook custard powder with milk & sugar until thick, layer with fruits, chill and serve.

Chocolate Brownie
Ingredients:
  • Butter - 100g
  • Chocolate - 100g
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup
  • Flour - 1/2 cup
  • Eggs - 2
Recipe:

Melt butter & chocolate, mix with sugar, eggs, and flour, bake at 180°C for 20-25 min.

Caramel Creme Delight
Ingredients:
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Cornflour - 2 tbsp
  • Vanilla essence - 1 tsp
Recipe:

Caramelize sugar, add milk & cornflour, cook until thick, add vanilla, pour into cups, chill.

Sheer Korma
Ingredients:
  • Milk - 4 cups
  • Vermicelli - 1/2 cup
  • Sugar - 1/2 cup
  • Dry fruits & nuts - 1/2 cup
  • Cardamom - 1 tsp
Recipe:

Boil milk, fry vermicelli in ghee, add to milk with sugar, cardamom, and dry fruits. Simmer until creamy.

Kheer
Ingredients:
  • Rice - 1/4 cup
  • Milk - 2 cups
  • Sugar - 1/4 cup
  • Cardamom powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Almonds & pistachios - 2 tbsp
Recipe:

Boil rice in milk on low heat, add sugar & cardamom, simmer until creamy, garnish with nuts.
