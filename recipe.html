Mala Hotpot Bowl

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Tofu cubes
  • Mushrooms
  • Chili oil
  • Garlic & ginger
  • Vegetable stock

Method

  1. Heat oil and sauté garlic & ginger.
  2. Add chili oil and vegetables.
  3. Pour stock and simmer.
  4. Add noodles and cook until soft.
  5. Serve hot with toppings.
