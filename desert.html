Gulab Jamun

Ingredients
  • 1 cup milk powder
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • Milk (as needed)
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, cardamom
Instructions
  1. Mix milk powder, flour, and ghee.
  2. Add milk to form soft dough.
  3. Make smooth balls.
  4. Fry on low flame until golden.
  5. Boil sugar, water & cardamom to make syrup.
  6. Soak balls for 2 hours before serving.

Kheer

Ingredients
  • 1 liter milk
  • 1/4 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • Cardamom, nuts
Instructions
  1. Boil milk.
  2. Add soaked rice.
  3. Cook on low flame, stirring.
  4. Add sugar and cardamom.
  5. Garnish with nuts and chill.

Gajar Ka Halwa

Ingredients
  • 1 kg carrots
  • 1 liter milk

  • 1/4 cup ghee
Instructions
  1. Cook carrots in milk.

  3. Add sugar and ghee.
  4. Cook till glossy.
